2. Cancellation Rules

Cancellation charges and refund eligibility are determined entirely by the specific bus operator’s policy, which is displayed to you at the time of booking. By proceeding with a booking, you agree to the operator's specific terms.

  • Cancellations must be made through the Alfabus app or website before the designated cut-off time.
  • No cancellations or refunds are permitted after the bus has departed its first boarding point.
  • If a passenger fails to report to the boarding point on time (No-Show), no refund will be issued.

4. Refunds for Operator Cancellations

Alfabus is not responsible for any incidental expenses (e.g., hotel, secondary travel) incurred due to the cancellation.
